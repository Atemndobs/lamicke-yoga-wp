About Me

Lamicke's Philosophy

My Philosophy is to encourage you to do something just for yourself.

I will like to help you connect mind and body in a way that best suits you. Find the right balance between the physical, mental and spiritual aspects of yoga.

My goal is to support you cultivate and sustain your own yoga- and healthy lifestyle and feel your best as you do this.

Strong Body

Increased Flexibility

Not only in your physical body but also in your way of thinking and being as well

Healthy Lifestyle

Consciously investing time in yourself, committing to your wellbeing

Get Rid Of Stress

My Offer to You

Join our worldwide community and let's spread love and light. Be love and light first for yourself and a source for all others who interact with you

At Lamicke Yoga I help you connect with yourself, become more present and aware through yoga and other mindfulness techniques.

I have benefited greatly from my yoga practice and being a yoga teacher is my way of giving back, my sadhana. In January 2021, I completed my 500-hour hatha yoga teacher training and am a certified yoga teacher with the European Federation of Yoga Teachers. I have been actively teaching in various settings since 2018 and practicing yoga is part of my daily routine.

Here you have a lot of yoga sessions to choose from 7 days a week Whether online or in presence. Whether you're a beginner or advanced, there's something for everyone here.

Having fun and making yoga poses accessible is an important part of what I offer. 

I look forward to seeing you on the mat.
